FOG  —  dim any web page and control its volume
================================================

Fog puts a tiny copper control panel on your web pages. The left (silver) knob
dims or brightens the page; the right (gold) knob lowers that tab's audio. Each
browser tab has its own panel, so you can dim one page or quiet one video
without touching the others.

Works in Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and other Chrome-based browsers, on Mac,
Windows, and Linux.


INSTALL  (about 2 minutes, one time)
------------------------------------
1. Unzip the file you received. You'll get a folder named "fog".
   Put it somewhere you won't delete it (e.g. your Documents folder).

2. Open your browser and go to the extensions page:
      - Chrome:  type  chrome://extensions  in the address bar and press Enter
      - Edge:    type  edge://extensions    in the address bar and press Enter

3. Turn on "Developer mode."
      - Chrome: a switch in the TOP-RIGHT corner.
      - Edge:   a switch on the LEFT side.
   (This switch is safe. It just lets you add an extension from a folder
    instead of the store.)

4. Click "Load unpacked," then select the "fog" folder from step 1.
   Fog now appears in your list with a little amber gem icon.

5. (Recommended) Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon near the address bar,
   then click the pushpin next to Fog so its icon stays visible.

A note you may see: the browser will say Fog can "read and change your data on
websites." That's expected — dimming a page means Fog has to run on the page.
It does not collect or send anything anywhere. It only changes brightness and
volume, right there in your browser.


HOW TO USE
----------
- Click the Fog icon to turn it ON. A small "ON" badge appears on the icon and
  the control panel shows up on your pages.
- Left knob  = brightness/contrast. Middle is normal; left dims, right brightens.
- Right knob = volume for that tab's audio/video. Slide left to quiet it.
- Move the panel: grab it anywhere except the knobs and drag it where you like.
- Click the Fog icon again to turn everything OFF.

The on/off choice sticks — through page refreshes, new tabs, and even after you
close and reopen your browser — until you turn it off again.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- No panel on a page? A few pages can't be touched by any extension (the new-tab
  page, the extensions page, the web store). Try a normal website.
- Just installed and an already-open tab has no panel? Refresh that tab once.
- Volume knob not affecting sound? It controls audio/video that lives on the page
  itself; sound from inside some embedded players may not respond.
